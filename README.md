\# MootCheck



MootCheck is an Instagram mutual checker that uses Playwright to compare your followers and following lists.



\## Features



\- Detects your logged-in Instagram account

\- Handles Instagram login and manual CAPTCHA/verification

\- Collects followers and following

\- Finds mutual followers

\- Finds accounts that don't follow you back

\- Finds accounts you don't follow back

\- Includes a CustomTkinter GUI

\- Can be packaged as a standalone Windows executable



\## Requirements



\- Windows

\- Python 3.12+

\- An Instagram account
